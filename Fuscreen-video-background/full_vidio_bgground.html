Animal World is a landmark documentary series by CCTV, launching on December 31, 1981. For over 40 years, it has brought the wonders of wildlife into Chinese households, with vivid footage and authoritative commentary. From the African savanna’s elephants and lions to the Amazon’s exotic birds and insects, and from polar bears in the Arctic to mysterious deep-sea creatures, the program reveals animals’ survival strategies, social behaviors, and their fragile connections to ecosystems. Beyond entertainment, it emphasizes conservation, urging respect for all life and harmony between humans and nature. Its iconic theme music and Zhao Zhongxiang’s legendary voice have made it a cultural touchstone across China.
